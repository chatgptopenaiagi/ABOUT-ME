# Ideas and Concepts

[Home](README.md) · [Repository index](INDEX.md) · [My vision](MY-VISION.md)

These ideas guide GENESIS. They combine established techniques with my choices about serving human objectives. I do not claim exclusive invention of the underlying methods.

**Implemented** means a bounded mechanism appears in recorded engineering work. **Exploration** and **aspiration** describe broader goals. Technical summaries reflect reports dated 26–27 October 2026, rather than fresh tests by this documentation repository.

| Idea | Meaning in my work | Evidence and present boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Human-hardware-aware AI** | Consider human objectives alongside actual hardware, software and constraints. | **Partly implemented:** the capability registry exposes machine conditions. General understanding of every human preference and environment remains an exploration. |
| **Persistent software capability memory** | Retain what is installed, where it runs, what was tested and what remains uncertain. | **Implemented:** GENESIS-3 records capabilities and dated verification evidence. Records must be checked again when relevant conditions change. |
| **Intelligent software discovery** | Find suitable existing tools before installing replacements. | **Implemented in bounded form:** GENESIS-3.1 queries capabilities and availability, limited to its inventory, relationships and evidence. |
| **AI-directed software construction** | Translate human-defined objectives into components, implementations, tests and repairs. | **Demonstrated through Codex-assisted work:** the GENESIS projects contain verified local results. This does not establish unrestricted autonomous engineering. |
| **Cross-application capability composition** | Combine tools through suitable interfaces. | **Partly implemented:** the engine proposes combinations with their dependencies; selected workflows were tested. Each new combination needs end-to-end verification. |
| **Predictive dependency reasoning** | Identify likely version, architecture, runtime and package conflicts. | **Implemented as bounded checks:** explicit constraints and recorded conditions guide predictions. Unknown compatibility remains unresolved. |
| **Causal engineering error memory** | Preserve observed failures, diagnosed causes, repairs and prevention rules. | **Implemented:** recorded cases distinguish confirmed, probable and unknown causes. A successful repair is evidence for its recorded conditions. |
| **Human-time recovery through automation** | Reduce repetitive technical labour and create more room for exploration and decisions. | **Aspiration:** reducing manual work motivates the projects. No general time-saving percentage or productivity improvement is claimed. |
| **AI-native financial software interfaces** | Give humans and agents structured access to a common financial-control system. | **Implemented in simulation:** GENESIS-4 exposes account, payment, ledger and reporting operations through an API and MCP tools. Real financial-provider connections remain future work. |
| **Continuity between independent AI sessions** | Let later agents recover context from reliable records instead of repeating every investigation. | **Partly implemented:** instructions, reports and persistent evidence support reuse. Continuity depends on access to those artifacts and renewed checks, not continuous model identity or automatic training. |

Each new task should benefit from previous work through accurate attribution, explicit limits and a clear distinction between ideas and tested results.
